Privacy

Short, because the site collects very little. The parts that matter are what we never hold at all.

What we never collect

No passwords. Sign-in goes through an identity provider, so a credential never reaches this site.

No payment details. There is nothing to pay for.

No advertising or cross-site trackers. There is no ad network, no pixel and no third-party analytics script on any page.

What we do collect

An email address, if you sign in, supplied by your identity provider along with your first name. It identifies your account and nothing else.

The questions you ask the assistant, and its answers. For a signed-in account these are kept as your history. Anonymous questions are kept without any identifier attached, to see what people want and where the model falls short.

Your IP address, briefly and in memory only, to count free questions. It is not written to a database and does not survive a restart. Server logs record only whether a request arrived through our network edge, not the address itself.

Who else sees it

Google, if you sign in with it, under their own privacy policy.

Anthropic, which turns the measurements into a sentence. Your question and the retrieved figures are sent; your email and account are not.

Cloudflare, which serves the pages and sees request metadata.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long it is kept

Account records last until you ask us to delete them. A sign-in session expires after 60 days. Session tokens are stored only as a one-way hash, so a copy of our database does not let anyone sign in as you.

Your choices

Write to hello@slatestats.com to see what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted. Deleting an account removes the address and the saved history.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR. We will honour an access or deletion request regardless of where you are.

Children

The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Betting content is intended for adults of legal age.
