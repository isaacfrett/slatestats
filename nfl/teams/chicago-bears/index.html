What this staff chooses

The Chicago Bears run more than the situation calls for: pass rate over expectation -3.0%, 20th of 32, on 64 plays a game (3rd). Raw pass rate is 50.3%, but that follows the scoreboard -- a trailing team throws. PROE subtracts the league rate for the same down, distance and score, so what is left is the CHOICE.

What this defense concedes

Of the passes thrown at the Chicago Bears, 63% go to receivers, 22% to tight ends and 15% to backs. Opponents average 9.40 yards a target (31st).

Read this half with more caution than the half above. How a defense DISTRIBUTES the targets it faces repeats moderately — 0.34 for backs, 0.23 for receivers, 0.09 for tight ends. How many YARDS it allows a tight end per target repeats at 0.02, which is indistinguishable from chance. "Weak against tight ends" is the least reproducible claim in this dataset and one of the most repeated.
