What this staff chooses

The Los Angeles Chargers run more than the situation calls for: pass rate over expectation -5.3%, 23rd of 32, on 58 plays a game (16th). Raw pass rate is 53.4%, but that follows the scoreboard -- a trailing team throws. PROE subtracts the league rate for the same down, distance and score, so what is left is the CHOICE.

Pass rate over expectation is the most repeatable offensive trait we measure: split the season into odd and even weeks and a team agrees with itself at 0.84. Most things a defense does sit near 0.32.

What this defense concedes

Of the passes thrown at the Los Angeles Chargers, 54% go to receivers, 25% to tight ends and 21% to backs. Opponents average 7.91 yards a target (16th).

Read this half with more caution than the half above. How a defense DISTRIBUTES the targets it faces repeats moderately — 0.34 for backs, 0.23 for receivers, 0.09 for tight ends. How many YARDS it allows a tight end per target repeats at 0.02, which is indistinguishable from chance. "Weak against tight ends" is the least reproducible claim in this dataset and one of the most repeated.
