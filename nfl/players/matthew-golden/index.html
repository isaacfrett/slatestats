“Cleared it recently” is his own record against today’s number over his last eight games, which can cross a season boundary. It describes the PAST: the book has seen the same games, so a high rate is not by itself an edge. The line is the consensus number posted for an upcoming game, captured and refreshed through the week. It is shown because it is the most useful thing to compare a projection against, not as a price: Slate Stats publishes no odds, takes no bets, and none of this is advice.
