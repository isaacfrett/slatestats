Who keeps clearing the number

A projection is one number and hides its own shape. Two players can both project four receptions — one catching 4, 5, 4, 5 and the other 0, 9, 1, 8 — and for anyone reading a line that difference is the whole question. This page shows the record behind the number, over each player’s last 8 games.

Against the number a book is actually asking

Every player with a line posted for an upcoming game. Notice how tightly these cluster around half. That is not a coincidence and it is not us failing to find anything: a book sets its line near the middle of a player’s distribution, so by construction he clears it about half the time. On this week’s board the highest rate of 396 priced players is 88%. Anywhere you see “hit in 8 of his last 10” quoted as an edge, this is the baseline it needs to beat.
