Slate Stats › NFL › My team

Start or sit, for your team

Type your Sleeper username and pick a league. You get our projections for the players you actually own, ranked, with the ones on reserve or carrying a designation flagged — instead of looking each of them up. Underneath, everybody who is free in that league: the waiver wire with the players somebody else already has taken out of it.

Nothing leaves your browser. Your browser talks to Sleeper directly and joins the result against a file on this site. There is no account, no password, and no server here that could store a roster even if we wanted it to — this site is static files. Your username is a public read on Sleeper’s own API.

Or an ESPN league

Paste your ESPN league ID — it is the leagueId= number in the URL when you are looking at your league. Public leagues only — a private league cannot be read from another site at all*.

We never see your ESPN login. For a private league your browser sends its own ESPN session directly to ESPN, the same way it does on espn.com. Nothing is pasted here, nothing is stored, and there is no server on this site that could hold it. Most tools ask you to copy your espn_s2 and SWID cookies out of developer tools — you should not have to, and here you do not.
