Your week, already decided

Type your Sleeper username once. You get a lineup, already set in your league’s own scoring, with every change from what you currently have named; the waiver claim worth making, as an add and a drop with the points it is worth; and every roster in the league ranked against the standings. Come back next week and it is on screen before you ask.

Nothing leaves your browser. Your browser talks to Sleeper directly and joins the result against a file on this site. There is no account, no password, and no server here that could store a roster even if we wanted it to — this site is static files. Your username is a public read on Sleeper’s own API. Which league you picked is kept in this browser so the answer is ready next time; it is stored on your device, not ours, and there is a link to forget it next to your leagues.

Or an ESPN league

Paste your ESPN league ID — it is the leagueId= number in the URL when you are looking at your league. Public leagues only — a private league cannot be read from another site at all*.

We never see your ESPN login. For a private league your browser sends its own ESPN session directly to ESPN, the same way it does on espn.com. Nothing is pasted here, nothing is stored, and there is no server on this site that could hold it. Most tools ask you to copy your espn_s2 and SWID cookies out of developer tools — you should not have to, and here you do not.
