What this number is worth

Catch rate is mostly a property of the THROWS, not the catcher: a player fed short, high-percentage targets will post a high rate whoever he is. Good hands are real; they are a small part of this number.

Do not treat a high catch rate as a skill you can project forward, and be suspicious of any argument that leans on it. The usage underneath -- targets, target share, snaps -- repeats four to five times better.
