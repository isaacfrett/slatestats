Who is on the field more

Snap share reproduces at 0.96 between halves of a season — receptions manage 0.89 and touchdowns 0.55. It is the earliest honest signal that a role has changed, and it moves before the box score does.

The biggest moves, drawn

A bar is points of snap share, not a percentage change: +12 means he was on the field for twelve more snaps out of every hundred than he had been. Players carrying an injury designation are labelled.
