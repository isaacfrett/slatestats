NFL red zone stats, 2026: offenses, defenses and who gets the touches

Every team’s trips inside the opponent’s 20 and how many ended in a touchdown, for and against, through week 4 of 2026 (4 games per team, 420 red-zone trips in all). These are raw rates, not adjusted for the opponents faced, so the order describes the season so far rather than rating anyone. A red-zone touchdown rate on a dozen trips barely repeats; touchdowns per drive and the players who get the ball near the goal line repeat far better.

What repeats in the red zone

Getting the ball there is a role; scoring with it repeats far less. A running back’s share of his team’s opportunities inside the 20 carries over across a season at 0.81. For wide receivers it is 0.62 and for tight ends 0.66, so a “red-zone receiver” changes more from week to week than a goal-line back. Touchdown counts in the tables above are what happened; the touches and targets beside them are the part that predicts.

For teams, the same split: reaching the red zone and scoring per drive are offensive traits, while the share of red-zone trips that become touchdowns is close to noise for offenses and defenses alike. The reliability of each figure is printed under its table.
