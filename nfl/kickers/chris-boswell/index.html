Kickers are close to unprojectable, and pretending otherwise would be the dishonest part. Field-goal attempts reproduce at 0.14 between halves of a season — an attempt happens when a drive stalls in range, and where drives stall is not a property of the kicker. The model beats simply quoting the league average by about 1%.
