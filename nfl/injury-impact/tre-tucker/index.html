If Tre Tucker is out, who gets his targets?

The Las Vegas Raiders room absorbs +2.69 receptions, and Jalen Nailor takes the largest single share at +0.62. The work stays overwhelmingly inside the wide receiver room.

The room total is predictable. Which player takes it is not. Every candidate predictor of who gains — pecking order, snap share, route depth, trailing targets — correlates at roughly r = 0.02. In any single week one player usually takes about 60% of what was vacated; it is simply not predictably the same one.
